CSR project proposal · submitted 12 April 2026

Families already own the goats. This project builds the enterprise around them.

A 24-month collective, shed-based goat enterprise for 120 marginalized families in Lakhimpur Kheri. CARD implements. IGL funds infrastructure, veterinary care, the FPO, licences and market access. No goat procurement.

July 2026 – June 2028 Nighasan · Pallia · Gola Gokaran Nath · Mohammadi Submitted to IGL CSR
Budget requested ₹58 lakh

100% IGL. Funds go to four scientific sheds, care, training, FPO formation, branding and ONDC — not to buying animals the families already keep.

Families120Women SHG members, SC/ST, BPL, landless and women-headed households. About 600 people.
Existing goats240Two per family, brought into four common sheds. Health-checked before entry.

What changes

The intervention is housing, health, feed and a market — not a new herd. Families contribute their goats and daily labour. Profit is shared quarterly after costs.

Why Lakhimpur Kheri

Largest district in Uttar Pradesh, Terai, Nepal border. Goat keeping is already widespread among landless and women-headed homes, and still stuck at subsistence.

Population~40 lakh88.5% rural. Literacy 60.56%.
Goat population3.19 lakh+Main work is farming and animal husbandry.
RiskFloodSharda, Gomti, Ghaghra and Sarayan. Sheds are the climate buffer.

24 months, three phases

01

Prepare

Months 1–4. Select 120 families, health-check goats, identify four cluster sites, build sheds, register the producer company and open its bank account.

02

Start

Months 5–9. Seven-day residential training, shift goats, vaccinate, insure all 240, issue starter feed, establish fodder plots, begin daily FPO management.

03

Sell

Months 10–24. Monthly camps, quarterly payouts, FSSAI, trade, ONDC and GST, brand and QR tags, then hand the enterprise to the FPO.

Who implements

CARD, Lucknow. Registered society, 12A and 80G, CSR-1, PAN and GST. Twenty-five years, with goat training at B. Sahar and Bakshi ka Talab, and a 2019 seminar at BAMETI, Patna.

Why this is fundable

Capital stays useful

Sheds are permanent. The proposal puts their life at 10+ years. Families already own the productive asset.

Exit is designed

Month 24 the FPO owns operations. Convergence lined up with Animal Husbandry, NABARD, SFAC and the PM FPO scheme.

Visible for IGL

Shed boards, pack branding and QR pages carry IGL CSR. Quarterly finance and photo reports, plus a live dashboard.
